Frequently Asked Questions

Is MarineoClash safe from bans?

Yes. MarineoClash works entirely from the outside using OpenCV computer vision to see your screen and virtual humanized mouse movements to click. It never injects code into the game process, never intercepts packets, and leaves zero footprint.

How do I receive my license key after paying?

The instant your Stripe checkout completes, our backend automatically generates your unique license key and delivers it immediately to your email address. Simply paste it into the 🔑 License tab in the app to activate.

Which emulators and platforms are supported?

MarineoClash supports official Google Play Games for PC natively, as well as popular Android emulators like LDPlayer and BlueStacks.

Can I transfer my license to a new PC?

Yes! Pro license keys support hardware migration. If you switch computers or reinstall Windows, you can reactivate your license key on your new device.
