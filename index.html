Frequently Asked Questions

Why is MarineoClash safe for main accounts?

MarineoClash does not require root or ADB debugging bridges. It runs strictly through the official Google Play Games for PC client, reading the screen externally using computer vision and emulating humanized mouse inputs. It leaves zero traces in game memory or network packets.

Is there a free trial / version available?

Yes! You can join our Discord Community to test the free version on your PC before purchasing a Pro license.

How do I receive my license key after purchasing?

As soon as your Stripe payment completes, our Supabase licensing server generates your Ed25519-signed key and emails it directly to your inbox. Simply paste the key in the 🔑 License tab in the MarineoClash app to activate.

Can I manage multiple Clash of Clans accounts?

Yes! MarineoClash includes an automated account rotation engine that smoothly cycles through your Supercell ID profiles, performs farm runs and village upgrades, and rotates automatically 24/7.
